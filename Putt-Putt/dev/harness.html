Putt Putt harness

Stands in for Barrelo while you build. It keeps the visit log exactly the way the host does and posts the same barrelo:gameState message into your board.

Aim (autoscorer)

Click the board to throw at the exact point you clicked. This is the continuous path a real autoscorer produces — the bull is the ball, so the vector from the centre to your click is the putt. Direction and power are both taken from where you click.

 

Aim (manual entry)

The typed-in path. Positions snap to the centre of the segment/ring wedge, so these buttons give the four quantised power levels most matches will actually see.
